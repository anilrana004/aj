# The Rudra Stone

**Slug:** `raw-garnet-centerpiece`
**Slot:** Centerpiece
**Material:** Raw Garnet
**Price:** ₹45,000
**Craft Time:** 5 days, bezel hand-formed to stone
**Origin:** Tonk, Rajasthan

## Story

Raw-cut garnet from Rajasthan's ancient mines near Tonk — unpolished, uncalibrated, each stone chosen for the depth of its burgundy fire rather than its symmetry. Set in a hand-formed gold bezel that follows the stone's natural contour (no two settings are identical), the Rudra Stone catches light from within. Named for the fierce, focused energy of its color — this is not a quiet stone. It announces itself.
