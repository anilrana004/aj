## Story

Natural rudraksha beads — the dried seeds of the Elaeocarpus ganitrus tree, sacred in Hindu tradition. Each bead has a unique surface texture (mukhi, or faces), and we select only 5-mukhi beads for our malas — the most common and spiritually significant variety. Strung on silk thread with hand-tied knots between each bead, 108 in total. The beads darken with use, absorbing oil from the skin, becoming smoother and more lustrous over years of practice.
