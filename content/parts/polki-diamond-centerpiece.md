## Story

Polki — uncut, unpolished diamond, the oldest form of the gem, set in the kundan technique that dates to Mughal courts. Each stone is backed with gold foil to amplify its natural fire, then nestled into lac within a hand-formed gold frame. The Champa Pendant is named for the champak flower — its open, unhurried form mirrors the way a polki stone unfolds light. No two are alike. Each carries the specific geology of its origin, frozen in stone before any blade touched it.
