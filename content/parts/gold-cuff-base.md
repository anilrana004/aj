## Story

An open cuff of 22k gold, hammered from a single ingot over three days. The Ankusha — elephant goad, a symbol of controlled power — takes its name from the Rajasthani tradition of the gold arm cuff, worn by women of means as a sign of independence. The cuff is slightly sprung to grip the wrist, and its surface bears the honest marks of the hammer. No polish. No mirror finish. Just gold, as the earth made it.
