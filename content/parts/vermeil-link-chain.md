**Origin:** Jaipur, Rajasthan

## Story

Cast in 18k gold vermeil — a thick layer of gold bonded over sterling silver — the Jaipur Link chain draws its pattern from the traditional hansli necklaces worn by Rajasthani women for centuries. Each link is individually soldered by hand, a process that takes our karigars two full days for a single chain. The result is a chain with weight and warmth that belies its price — substantial in the hand, soft against skin, catching light like still water.
